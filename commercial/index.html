Safety

Code-Compliant Installation and Documentation

A poorly installed or neglected water heater can create carbon monoxide hazards, scalding, bacterial growth in lukewarm storage, and expensive water damage from a ruptured tank. Those are real risks for any commercial property owner with employees, customers, and tenants in the building. Every installation and inspection we perform covers venting, combustion air, gas line sizing, relief valve discharge, expansion tanks, mixing valves, and code items like drain pans and seismic straps where required, and each visit ends with a written report listing risks, priorities, and recommended preventive steps.

Good records matter almost as much as good repairs. Every account gets a documentation binder with detailed service logs, temperature readings, and relief valve test results, giving you proof of due diligence for landlords, insurers, and inspectors. Equipment that works hardest, in kitchens, laundries, and central plant rooms, gets a more frequent service schedule.

Emergency Service

Rapid-Response Water Heater Repair for Businesses

When a commercial water heater fails, the clock starts immediately. Dish rooms stall, salon chairs empty, guests call the front desk, and tenants start complaining. Calls to our commercial line get an experienced technician dispatched quickly for emergency repairs throughout Anderson and the surrounding area, and scheduled work can be done overnight or before opening to limit downtime.

Commercial emergencies come in many forms. The cause could be a failed gas valve, a burned-out element, a flame sensor that stopped proving flame, a tripped high-limit switch, a leaking tank, a stalled recirculation pump, or a tankless bank stuck in lockout. Whatever the cause, we find it fast, restore hot water, and record every step for your facility files. Then we put maintenance in place so the same failure doesn't return.

Preventive Care

Commercial Water Heater Maintenance Programs

Preventing failures is the most cost-effective way to manage commercial water heaters. Every Anderson Water Heater program is tailored to the property's equipment, usage, and budget, and covers scheduled inspections, flushing, anode rod checks, descaling for tankless units, relief valve tests, burner and vent inspection, recirculation and mixing valve checks, and verification of delivered water temperature.

Scheduled maintenance typically costs a fraction of an emergency replacement and sharply cuts the risk of downtime and tenant complaints. Properties on a program usually see fewer outages, longer equipment life, and more consistent water temperatures. Call us to discuss a plan designed for your property's needs and budget.
